# Tokenization-Aware Protein Language Modeling

Companion page for **ECCB 2026 poster C-P.51** in Geneva, Switzerland.

**Tokenization-Aware Protein Language Modeling with Evolution-Guided Units and Dynamic Biological Knowledge Fusion**

Amirreza Sattarzadeh · Burak Suyunu · Özdeniz Dolu · Arzucan Özgür  
LifeLU · TABILAB · Department of Computer Engineering, Boğaziçi University

## Study at a glance

We systematically evaluate protein tokenization, vocabulary size, fine-tuning strategy, and sequence-derived dynamic biological knowledge within a matched ESM-2 35M framework.

- Tokenizer families: PUMA variants, BPE, and amino-acid tokens
- Seven vocabulary scales
- Four fine-tuning regimes
- Fourteen downstream tasks from the PETA benchmark
- More than 7,000 training runs

The central finding is that there is no context-free “best” tokenizer. The practical choice depends on task granularity, data, vocabulary scale, fine-tuning strategy, and available compute.
